Applications arrive from **Indeed and downtoearth.org into one queue**. The
Applicant Screener reads each one and scores it 1–100 against the standards in
the [scoring rubric](/dashboard/playbooks/scoring-rubric); the Store Router
sends 70-and-above to the right store manager. Below 70 is **held with the
reason recorded — never auto-rejected**, and 60–69 near-misses are flagged for
a second look. Every number below is synthetic fixture data.
