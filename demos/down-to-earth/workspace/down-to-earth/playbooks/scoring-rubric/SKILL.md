# Status: demo baseline

**Everything in this rubric is Metacto's placeholder, built from the discovery call and the Aug 2026 proposal.** Setup week 1 replaces it: the criteria working sessions turn the real job descriptions and the criteria in managers' heads into a written, uniform standard per role and level. Amy is sending job descriptions + qualifications for the levels ("twos, threes, and fours"). Until then, treat scores as demonstrations of the mechanism, not the calibrated standard.

# Levels

| Level | Roles | Screens |
|---|---|---|
| **Level 2** (entry) | sales associate / sales clerk, cashier, deli clerk, produce clerk | store manager |
| **Level 3** (supervisor) | department supervisor, assistant manager, manager on duty, relief manager | store manager + corporate |
| **Level 4** (management) | store manager, deli manager | corporate leads |

# Criteria & weights

The five criteria from the proposal's "Scoring Standard" page, weighted per level.

## Level 2 — entry (the bulk: ~20 hires/month)

| Criterion | Weight | Strong | Weak |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer-facing experience (any industry) | 25 | 1+ yr retail/food service/hospitality | none, and no adjacent signal |
| Availability vs store coverage | 25 | weekends + evenings stated | weekday-only, or not stated (→ unknown) |
| Food handling readiness | 25 | food-handler card, deli counter, stocking | none |
| Reliability signals | 15 | 12+ months tenure at recent jobs, references offered | many short unexplained stints |
| Inventory / POS systems | 10 | register, POS, receiving, counts | none (a light minus, never disqualifying) |

## Level 3 — supervisor

Level 2 criteria, re-weighted (20/20/20/10/5), **plus supervisory experience (25)**: shift-lead or better, team size and duration stated. **No supervisory history at all caps the total at 60.**

## Level 4 — management

Requires **multi-year management of a team** and **operational scope** (P&L, inventory, scheduling, vendor contact). Missing either **caps the total at 55**. Grocery/food-retail management is the strongest signal; adjacent retail management is solid.

# Threshold & bands

**The threshold is 70** (proposal pp. 4 and 6). At or above 70 → routed to the manager; below 70 → held.

| Band | Meaning | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **85–100** | strong match | routed with priority ("interview?") |
| **70–84** | qualified | routed; low-70s presented as "your call" with gaps named |
| **60–69** | near miss | **held**, listed for corporate with score and reason — flagged as worth a second human look |
| **< 60** | below threshold | **held** — visible in the portal, reason recorded, never deleted, never auto-rejected; any hold can be reopened |

Thresholds are set per role and store at the criteria sessions and can be tightened any time — **confirm 70 with Down to Earth at kickoff**. "Held" always means *kept out of the manager's inbox and listed for corporate review*, never rejected.

# What never moves a score

Smoking/vaping answer (flag only — see `hiring-standards`) · plant-based acknowledgement gaps (collect, don't deduct) · work history in meat/fish/eggs contexts (that's experience) · any non-job-related attribute · which source or store the application came through.

# How the standard learns

Every review-queue decision (approve / adjust / decline, and the manager's eventual hire) is captured. When a pattern has enough evidence and is job-related, the system **proposes** a rubric change in plain language — scoped, e.g. "Deli Clerk · all stores" — and **nothing changes until corporate approves it**. Approved changes apply to every store the same day and are versioned: recorded, attributed, reversible. History informs proposals; people change the rules. (Worked example: prior food-service *shift work* correlated with tenure in deli roles → approved slight up-weight for Deli Clerk levels 1–2 — see the `score_candidate` learnings.)
