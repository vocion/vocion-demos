# Status: demo baseline

**Everything in this rubric is Metacto's placeholder, built from the discovery call.** The engagement's weeks 1–3 replace it: working sessions with corporate and managers turn the real job descriptions and the criteria in managers' heads into a written, uniform rubric per role. Amy is sending job descriptions + qualifications for the levels ("twos, threes, and fours"). Until then, treat scores as demonstrations of the mechanism, not the calibrated standard.

# Levels

| Level | Roles | Screens |
|---|---|---|
| **Level 2** (entry) | sales associate / sales clerk, cashier, deli clerk | store manager |
| **Level 3** (supervisor) | department supervisor, assistant manager, manager on duty, relief manager | store manager + corporate |
| **Level 4** (management) | store manager, deli manager | corporate leads |

# Criteria & weights

## Level 2 — entry (the bulk: ~20 hires/month)

| Criterion | Weight | Strong | Weak |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer-facing experience (any industry) | 30 | 1+ yr retail/food service/hospitality | none, and no adjacent signal |
| Availability match | 25 | weekends + evenings stated | weekday-only, or not stated (→ unknown) |
| Food/grocery specifics | 25 | food-handler card, POS, stocking, deli counter | none |
| Reliability signals | 20 | 12+ months tenure at recent jobs, references offered | many short unexplained stints |

## Level 3 — supervisor

Level 2 criteria, re-weighted (20/20/20/15), **plus supervisory experience (25)**: shift-lead or better, team size and duration stated. **No supervisory history at all caps the total at 60.**

## Level 4 — management

Requires **multi-year management of a team** and **operational scope** (P&L, inventory, scheduling, vendor contact). Missing either **caps the total at 55**. Grocery/food-retail management is the strongest signal; adjacent retail management is solid.

# Bands & threshold

| Band | Meaning | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **85–100** | strong match | routed with priority |
| **70–84** | solid match | routed |
| **50–69** | borderline | routed, gaps named for the manager |
| **< 50** | below threshold | **held from routing** — visible in the portal, never deleted, never auto-rejected; a human can always pull one back up |

The ~50 threshold is from Chris's proposed design on the call ("anything below a 50 just gets filtered out") — **confirm the number with Down to Earth at the criteria sessions**, and note that "filtered" means *held from the manager's queue*, not rejected.

# What never moves a score

Smoking/vaping answer (flag only — see `hiring-standards`) · plant-based acknowledgement gaps (collect, don't deduct) · work history in meat/fish/eggs contexts (that's experience) · any non-job-related attribute · which source or store the application came through.

# Calibration loop

Every review-queue decision (approve / adjust / decline, and the manager's eventual hire) feeds back as a learning. When managers repeatedly adjust scores in one direction for one role, that's the rubric asking to be re-weighted — raise it at the Day-60 review, don't silently drift.
