# Inspection standard (demo default — the manufacturer's own numbers replace these)

- **Pass**: every labelled outline on the silhouette sheet has its part, correctly oriented, at the printed quantity; nothing extra on the sheet. Reference-comparison confidence ≥ 0.90.
- **Hold**: any region missing, wrong part, mis-oriented (face-down, mirrored bracket), off-count, or unreadable; or an unexpected item on the sheet; or confidence < 0.90 on a pass. A hold keeps the kit at the station. It is not a rejection and not rework until a person says so.
- **Near-miss band**: confidence 0.80–0.90 on either verdict. The Quality Analyst tracks these; a hand check is cheap.
- **Unreadable**: a fastener box that cannot be counted at photo resolution, glare over a region, hands covering a region, the sheet not fully in frame. Report as a finding; recommend a hand count or a re-photo.
- **Confidence is spoken, not printed**: "clear", "likely", "unsure — hand-check". A decimal belongs in the record, not the sentence.
- **Zero defects is the goal; the promise is the capability plus a measured baseline.** Shadow mode first: record verdicts alongside the existing process before any verdict gates a shipment.
- **Small labelled set caveat**: the classifier was trained on dozens of photos of two kits, and its Bad examples were staged at a second station. Treat its label as a second opinion.
