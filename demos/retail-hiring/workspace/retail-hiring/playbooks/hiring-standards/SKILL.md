# The two policies from the discovery call

These came directly from the HR lead (Jul 24, 2026 discovery call). They are encoded as **flags and routing**, never as automated rejections.

## 1. Plant-based on property

The stores are strictly plant-based. The policy, exactly as the company states it:

- Employees **do not** have to be vegan or vegetarian.
- Employees **must respect and promote** plant-based.
- **No meat, eggs, or fish come onto the property** — anyone's, including corporate. The reason is respect for customers and contamination risk.

**What this means for screening:**

- The application asks the applicant to acknowledge this policy. Acknowledged → noted. Missing → a **gap the Screener collects by follow-up**, not a deduction, not a rejection.
- **Work history is never a violation.** A butcher shop, a seafood counter, a fast-food job — that's food-handling experience, and often *relevant, positive* experience. Deducting for it is a scoring error. the HR lead eats chicken and seafood; the policy is about what comes on property.

## 2. Smoking / vaping — the CEO-approval path

The application asks: **"do you vape or smoke?"** The CEO is firm on this, and the exact mechanics matter:

- **Yes (or blank) → flag: "CEO approval required per policy before hire."** The flag rides with the applicant through scoring and routing.
- **The score is unaffected.** A 91 who smokes is still a 91 — with a flag.
- **The applicant still routes to the manager.** If the manager wants to advocate for them, the CEO decides. Per the HR lead: he has almost never said no — the condition is no smoking on property or around the food.
- **Auto-rejecting a smoker is the worst failure this system can produce.** It would automate away a decision the CEO explicitly reserves for himself.

# Fairness rules — every agent, every time

- **Job-related criteria only.** Score, route, and report on experience, availability, certifications, and supervisory scope. Age, gender, ethnicity, name, family status, neighborhood, photos: never extracted, never scored, never mentioned.
- **Consistent = the same rubric, weighting, and bands for every applicant, at every store, from every source.** The sell is consistency, not automation.
- **Unknown ≠ bad.** Missing information is collected, not penalized.
- **Nothing is ever deleted or silently rejected.** Below-threshold applicants are *held from routing* and stay visible in the portal. Declines are human decisions delivered by humans.
- **Managers decide.** The system reads, scores, routes, and explains. The interview and the hire belong to the store manager (with CEO sign-off where the flag requires it).

# Who screens what

- **Level 2 (entry)** — sales associate/clerk, cashier, deli clerk → store managers.
- **Level 3** — department supervisors, assistant manager, manager on duty, relief manager → store manager **+ corporate admin**.
- **Level 4** — store manager, deli manager → store manager (where applicable) **+ corporate admin**; corporate leads.

Corporate = the HR lead + the HR manager (⚠ unnamed — verify before any doc names him). Volume baseline: ~60 applicants/month, ~20 hires, mostly entry-level; management "a dozen or so periodically."
